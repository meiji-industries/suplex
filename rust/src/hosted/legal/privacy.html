Suplex privacy policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Meiji Heavy Industries, Inc., an Ontario corporation, provides Suplex and is responsible for personal information under its control. This policy covers our Suplex websites, application, account services, billing, and support.

Privacy contact: info@meiji.industries

If an employer or another organization operates your Suplex installation, it also controls information within that installation. Contact its administrator about its practices and your access. This policy describes our practices.

2. Information we handle

Accounts. We collect your email address, an optional name, account and installation identifiers, and authentication records. If you sign in through Google or GitHub, we receive your verified email and available profile name. If you sign in by email, our email provider handles delivery of the sign-in link.

Payments. Our payment and subscription providers process purchases. We handle customer and subscription identifiers, plan and seat information, transaction amounts and currency, and payment, invoice, and refund status to administer access and billing. Payment details entered in the payment provider's checkout are handled by that provider.

Product activity. When enabled, analytics can include account, installation, and organization identifiers, account email, application version, operating system, browser information, screens visited, workflow and provider categories, feature usage, counts, durations, and sanitized error information. These records can be linked to an account and should not be treated as anonymous.

Website activity. With the applicable analytics choice enabled, we collect page visits, selected link clicks, referral and campaign information, and download information. A visitor identifier may be stored in a first-party cookie for up to one year and linked to your account when you sign in. Hosted download events use a separate random event identity.

Support. We receive information you choose to send when you contact us, including messages and any files or diagnostic information you provide. Please remove secrets and unnecessary personal information before sending them.

Our websites and hosted services process technical information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect the services.

3. Code, prompts, and connected tools

Suplex operates on the machine or host chosen by you or your administrator. Connected agents and integrations may send code, prompts, files, and other context to their providers as part of the work you request. Those providers' terms and privacy policies govern their handling of that information.

Suplex's product analytics are configured to exclude code, prompts, task titles and descriptions, repository names, file paths, and company or project names. This analytics restriction does not prevent connected tools from processing content needed for a task or prevent you from sending content to support.

We do not use customer code, prompts, or generated work to train general-purpose AI models. This statement does not describe the independent practices of providers you connect.

4. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide requested services, administer subscriptions, send service messages, respond to support requests, maintain security, diagnose problems, and comply with legal obligations. Where enabled and permitted, analytics help us understand product use and improve Suplex.

We also use email addresses to send promotional messages, including product announcements and offers. Promotional emails are separate from account, security, and billing messages, and from sharing email addresses with advertising platforms for audience matching.

We send promotional emails only after you separately and explicitly opt in. This choice is unchecked by default and independent of accepting our terms, logging in, consenting to advertising cookies, or consenting to advertising audience uploads. Every promotional email includes an unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe without losing access to Suplex; necessary account, security, and billing messages remain separate.

We will explain a materially new purpose and obtain consent where required before using personal information for that purpose.

5. Analytics and your choices

An installation administrator controls Send usage data in Suplex's Environment settings. In builds configured for analytics, this setting is on by default, but sending starts only after the administrator responds to the initial notice. Turning it off stops future installation analytics. Ask your administrator about shared-installation settings.

Application session replay has a separate Record session replays setting that is off by default. When enabled and available, it records screen interactions with text and inputs masked and sensitive content areas excluded.

Website analytics use the website's analytics choice. Website screen recording, if enabled, is subject to that choice and uses masking. Application and website browser analytics respect supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. These signals do not disable information needed to provide an account or process a purchase.

Hosted account, billing, and download analytics have separate service-level controls. The installation's Send usage data switch does not switch these off. Hosted analytics may link sign-in activity, email, installation activity, and a consented website visitor identifier.

Contact info@meiji.industries to ask about hosted analytics or withdraw consent for optional uses of your information.

Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully performed. You can contact us about information already collected.

6. Who receives information

We use service providers for hosting, payments, subscription management, email, website delivery, and analytics. They receive information needed for their services. Some providers also act for their own purposes under their policies.

Our service providers include Stripe for payments, RevenueCat for subscription management, Resend for email, and PostHog for analytics. We use Fly.io, Cloudflare, and Framer for infrastructure and website services.

Google or GitHub receives information when you choose its sign-in service. Tools and integrations you connect receive information as needed for the actions you request. Your organization's administrators may access information within the installation they manage.

We may disclose information when legally required, to address fraud or security threats, or to protect legal rights, subject to applicable law. A corporate transaction involving personal information will be handled with the safeguards and notices required by law.

We use advertising pixels from Meta, Google, and other advertising platforms to measure advertising performance and show relevant advertisements, including advertisements to people who have visited our websites. These pixels share information about website visits and interactions with the advertising platforms, which process it under their own privacy policies.

We also share customer email addresses with Meta and Google to match customers to accounts on those platforms and create audiences for our advertising. These platforms process the information under their own privacy policies. We upload your email only after you separately and explicitly opt in to this use. This choice is unchecked by default and independent of accepting our terms, logging in, consenting to advertising cookies, or subscribing to marketing emails. The opt-in explains the advertising purpose and identifies Meta and Google as recipients. Hashing an email address does not make it anonymous.

We load advertising pixels only after you consent to advertising cookies. You can accept or reject optional cookies, or choose analytics and advertising separately in Manage preferences. You can withdraw consent at any time through Privacy choices. Rejecting advertising cookies does not prevent you from using Suplex. We apply your advertising choice to any server-side advertising events we send as well.

7. Processing locations and security

Our providers may process information outside your province or country, where local laws may allow access by courts, law enforcement, or other authorities.

We use safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of personal information and remain responsible for information transferred to providers for processing on our behalf. No system can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you suspect a privacy or security incident.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including applicable accounting, legal, security, and dispute requirements. We then delete or de-identify it as appropriate.

We retain identifiable product analytics only as long as needed to understand usage and improve Suplex, then delete or anonymize them. The account-deletion commitment below also applies to identifiable product analytics.

Logging out or uninstalling Suplex does not delete your hosted account or cancel a subscription. Contact us to request account closure or deletion. Within 30 days of your account deletion, we delete your personal information from our active systems, except records we must retain to meet legal obligations. We will explain applicable exceptions.

We do not store backups of your Suplex installation or project data. You or your administrator manage those backups and their retention. Account deletion does not remove files or backups held on your own host or information independently held by connected providers.

9. Access, correction, and complaints

You can contact our privacy contact to ask about our practices, request access to or correction of your personal information, request deletion, or withdraw consent where applicable. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law.

We may verify your identity using information proportionate to the request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or another privacy regulator with jurisdiction.

Suplex is intended for people who have reached the age of majority where they live. If you believe we collected a child's information contrary to this policy, contact us.

10. Policy changes

We will post an updated date when this policy changes and provide notice of material changes. Where a change requires consent, we will obtain it before applying the new practice. Posting a policy does not itself establish consent.
