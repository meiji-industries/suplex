Suplex support

Send questions and problem reports to info@meiji.industries.

Tell us the Suplex version, your operating system, and the steps that cause the problem. Do not send passwords, API keys, or other secrets.

Phone app

Suplex AI on iPhone, iPad, and Android shows a Suplex host that you or your organization runs. The app does not run agents on the phone.

The host address must start with https://. If the host uses Tailscale, start Tailscale on the phone before you connect. To change the host, tap the host bar at the bottom of the screen.

If you cannot sign in to a host that your organization runs, ask its administrator for an invitation.
